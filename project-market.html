The Challenge

Zhanjiang has strong coastal tourism and fishery resources, yet consumer-oriented research on its recreational-fisheries sector remained limited. Our goal was to understand what actually drives visitor satisfaction, identify the most urgent experience gaps, and convert the findings into practical recommendations for operators, industry organizations and policymakers.

RESEARCH QUESTION

What matters most to recreational-fishery visitors — and where should the industry improve first?

Findings & Strategy

01 / SATISFACTION

Positive — but not frictionless.

Consumers were generally satisfied, but the gap between expectations and perceived experience pointed to clear room for improvement.

02 / DRIVER

Perceived quality is the primary lever.

Perceived quality carried the largest weight in the evaluation framework, pointing to the on-site consumer experience as a central improvement priority.

03 / IMPROVEMENT PRIORITIES

Not every problem deserves equal attention.

Priority Areas
  • Dining & accommodation convenience
  • Signage and wayfinding
  • Fishing-village cultural experience
  • Shopping environment
Existing Strengths
