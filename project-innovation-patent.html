Initial Concept Contributor · First-Listed Inventor

The Challenge

A practical records-storage problem identified through finance work: keeping physical documents organised while making the storage unit easier to handle.

My Contribution

Proposed the initial utility-model concept, collaborated with engineering teammates on functional design and helped prepare patent documentation.

Granted utility-model patent · ZL 2023 2 2544773.3 · 14 May 2024

The official certificate lists TAN Can first among five inventors. Guangdong Ocean University is the patent holder.

These projects strengthened my ability to connect practical needs with product thinking, interdisciplinary collaboration and tangible outcomes.
