TRACK 01 / NEURAL DYNAMICS

Inside ACGND

How can high-dimensional, time-varying tensor inversion be solved quickly enough for dynamic AI and engineering systems?

Dynamic Tensor Inversion is relevant to tensor-based AI frameworks such as TensorFlow and PyTorch. Conventional numerical methods can incur lagging errors in dynamic settings; neural-dynamics approaches address this challenge, with different trade-offs in accuracy and computational cost.

O(n³)O(n²)

The research asks how an evolving tensor can be inverted while keeping pace with a dynamic system.

Conceptual illustration · Complexity improvement reported by the research stream
