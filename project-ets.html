2022RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Mapping the Evolution of Research on China’s Marine Intangible Cultural Heritage

FIRST AUTHOR

MANUSCRIPT IN REVISION · PREPARING FOR RESUBMISSION

Prior Review History: External peer review at Cultural Heritage (CSSCI)

My first systematic research project, built around intensive literature reading, bibliometric mapping and academic synthesis. The work examined two decades of Chinese research on marine intangible cultural heritage and established the literature-review and research-writing foundation that later supported my empirical work.

1,000+Papers reviewed
2003–2022Research window
3Major Chinese databases

CiteSpace · Bibliometric Analysis · Knowledge Mapping · NoteExpress

ABSTRACT PREVIEW

Using literature from CNKI, Wanfang and VIP covering 2003–2022, this study maps the evolution of China’s marine intangible-cultural-heritage research through bibliometric and qualitative analysis. It identifies the field’s major research clusters around preservation, inheritance and individual heritage cases, while highlighting emerging themes including rural revitalisation, living heritage transmission and digital preservation.

System Dynamics of Fishermen’s Livelihood Resilience in Guangdong

Simulation and Analysis of Fishermen’s Livelihood Resilience in Guangdong Province in the Context of “Remote Utilization and Ecological Development”

CO-FIRST AUTHOR

MANUSCRIPT IN REVISION · PREPARING FOR RESUBMISSION

Prior Review History: Previously advanced to final review at Advances in Earth Science (Peking University Core Journal)

Previous submission history

Developed under the guidance of my undergraduate supervisor, this project marked my transition from research synthesis into formal empirical modelling. I used system dynamics to translate a conceptual livelihood-resilience framework into a dynamic model of how learning capacity, buffering capacity and self-organisation interact under disaster risk and fisheries-policy intervention.

System Dynamics · Vensim · Scenario Simulation · Livelihood Resilience · Policy Analysis

ABSTRACT PREVIEW

This study develops a three-dimensional livelihood-resilience framework to examine the endogenous relationships among learning, buffering and self-organisation capacities in Guangdong’s fishing communities. Using system-dynamics simulation, it further evaluates how alternative policy scenarios under offshore expansion and ecological-development strategies may reshape livelihood resilience under climate and disaster risks.

The Dark Side of Carbon Markets?

ETS-Induced Reduction in Corporate Patient Capital in China’s High-Pollution Industries

FIRST AUTHOR · SUBMITTED MANUSCRIPT

Submitted to Ecological Economics

Building on my earlier training in literature synthesis and empirical modelling, this study reflects a more independent and mature research agenda in environmental finance. It asks whether carbon pricing, while accelerating low-carbon transition, may simultaneously weaken the long-horizon capital firms need to finance that transition.

RESEARCH DESIGN

Staggered ETS Reform · Multi-Period DID · Firm Panel Data · Mechanism Analysis · Heterogeneity Analysis · Robustness & Placebo Tests

2007–2022Chinese A-share listed firms
RESEARCH SUMMARY

Using the staggered rollout of China’s emissions trading scheme as a quasi-natural experiment, this study examines whether carbon pricing affects firms’ accumulation of patient capital. The results identify a potential capital-structure cost of ETS regulation and explore the financial-risk and environmental-information channels through which the effect operates.
