CASE 01 / AVIATION SAFETY

Aviation Safety Risk & Flight-Technique Evaluation

2023 MathorCup Mathematical Modeling Challenge · Second Prize

Turning high-frequency flight data into interpretable safety signals, pilot-operation evaluation and automated warning logic.

The Question

How can QAR flight data be transformed into a practical framework for identifying key safety variables, quantifying unstable flight-control behavior and evaluating flight technique?

The project required working across heterogeneous flight parameters, outlier-prone measurements and multiple analytical objectives—from identifying safety-critical variables to evaluating pilot-operation patterns and designing a real-time warning mechanism.

Team competition study · research writing and analytical interpretation.

MY ROLE

Research Writing & Analysis Lead

Data & Analysis

Collected and preprocessed relevant flight data, supported empirical analysis and worked with the modeling member to interpret model outputs.

Research Integration

Connected assumptions, analytical procedures, figures and results into a coherent technical narrative across the project’s five modeling tasks.

Final Delivery

Led manuscript development, formatting, revision and final report integration, producing a technical submission including code.

CASE 04 / CONSUMER NLP

Consumer Review Intelligence with BERT–BiLSTM–CNN

2023 IMMCHE · Consumer-review mathematical modeling study

Moving from raw consumer text to product classification, sentiment signals, keyword interpretation and human–machine review detection.

The Question

How can unstructured review text be transformed into reliable signals about product categories, consumer sentiment, ratings and review authenticity?

The project treated consumer reviews as a multi-task NLP problem rather than a single sentiment-classification exercise. The workflow combined text preprocessing, semantic classification, keyword extraction, rating-related analysis and human–machine review discrimination.
