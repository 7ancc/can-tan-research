The Challenge

Citrus psyllids spread the disease known as huanglongbing (HLB), creating a persistent challenge for growers. The project connected earlier pest detection, usable field information and treatment hardware within one practical control workflow.

How can a technical pest-control capability become a usable, affordable product for citrus growers?

How the System Works

From field observations to a digital warning and targeted treatment.

DETECT

My Role

As Deputy Project Lead, I coordinated business-side execution across product, finance, field research, competitions and external engagement.

01

Commercial Strategy & Market Intelligence

Led the business workstream from field research and market sizing to customer segmentation, competitive analysis, product positioning and go-to-market strategy.

02

Business & Financial Modelling

Built the project’s commercial and financial framework, including revenue forecasts, cost structures, pricing assumptions, projected financial statements and investment analysis, while overseeing project-budget use and equipment-cost control.

03

Productisation

Worked with the technical team to translate AI recognition, pest monitoring and heat-treatment technologies into user scenarios, product configurations, pricing logic and commercially deployable service packages.

Coordinated field research, pilot deployment and external engagement with growers, cooperatives, enterprises and research institutes, feeding first-hand evidence back into product and commercial decisions.
